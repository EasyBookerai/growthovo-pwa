## Questions for You

### 1. Voice & Tone
**Does this feel right?**
- Direct, conversational, no corporate BS
- Punchy sentences, active voice
- Speaks TO the reader, not AT them
- Confident but not preachy

**Too aggressive? Too casual? Just right?**

### 2. Content Depth
**Is this the right level?**
- Concept cards: Hook → explanation → insight
- Examples: Person → struggle → action → outcome → reflection
- Mistakes: What people do → why it fails → the trap → alternative
- Science: Institution → finding → mechanism → translation
- Challenges: Trigger → specific action → time bound → reflection

**Too much detail? Not enough?**

### 3. Examples & Characters
**Do these feel real?**
- Names: Sofia, Liam, Emma, Mia (diverse, relatable)
- Ages: 19-24 (target demographic)
- Situations: Uni, early career, real struggles
- Outcomes: Specific timeframes, measurable changes

**Believable? Inspiring? Relatable?**

### 4. Science Quality
**Is the research level right?**
- Real institutions (UCLA, Harvard, Stanford, Carl Rogers)
- Specific findings (47% reduction, 28% premium, 30% faster)
- Mechanisms explained (amygdala, cortisol, prefrontal cortex)
- Practical translation (what this means for you)

**Too technical? Too simple? Credible?**

### 5. Challenges
**Are these doable?**
- Immediate triggers: "Right now," "In your next conversation"
- Specific actions: "Count 10 heartbeats," "Summarize before responding"
- Time-bound: "60 seconds," "Once today"
- Reflection built in: "Notice what happens"

**Too easy? Too hard? Motivating?**
