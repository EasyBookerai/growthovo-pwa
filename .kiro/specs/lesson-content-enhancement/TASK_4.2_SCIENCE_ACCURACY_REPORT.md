   - **Tier 3:** Mind pillar (best of the original 5, minor additions)

3. **Template for Strong Science Cards** (use Fitness as model):
   ```
   Research from [Institution] tracking [sample size] found that [specific finding with numbers]. The mechanism: [biological/psychological explanation]. [Practical translation].
   ```

   **Example (from Fitness Lesson 5):**
   "Harvard research tracking 139,000 people found that walking 30 minutes daily (roughly 3000-4000 steps) reduces all-cause mortality by 24% compared to sedentary individuals. The mechanism: walking activates muscle glucose uptake, improves endothelial function (blood vessel health), reduces systemic inflammation, and triggers BDNF release (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) which supports cognitive function and mood."

4. **Acceptable Citation Formats:**
   - "Research from [University] shows..."
   - "[Researcher name] at [Institution] found..."
   - "A study in [Journal] found..."
   - "Meta-analysis by [Researcher] shows..."
   - "[Framework name] by [Creator] explains..."

5. **When Specific Citations Aren't Available:**
   - Use "established principles in [field]" for well-known concepts
   - Use "consensus in [field] research" for widely accepted findings
   - Avoid "research shows" without qualification
