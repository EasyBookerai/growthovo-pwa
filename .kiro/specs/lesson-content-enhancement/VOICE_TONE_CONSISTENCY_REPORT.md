**Location:** Career Unit 3 (Negotiation lessons, 2 instances)

**Recommendation:** Consider alternatives:
- "Information is leverage" → "Information is power" or "Information gives you an edge"

#### Issue 3: "Implementation Intentions" (Mind Pillar)
**Location:** Mind pillar seed data (lessons_all_pillars.sql line 165)
**Text:** Title: "Implementation Intentions"

**Assessment:** This is an established psychological term (from Peter Gollwitzer's research), so it's acceptable in a **title and science card**. The concept card explains it in plain language: "When X happens, I will do Y."

**Recommendation:** No change needed - the academic term is immediately translated to accessible language.

#### Issue 4: "Optimize" in Career Context
**Location:** Career pillar, mentions in passing

**Assessment:** Appears 1-2 times in the context of career positioning research citations. Used in science cards citing research, not in concept/challenge/example cards.

**Assessment:** Sentence structure varies for rhythm but maintains brevity. Active voice dominates. No passive, meandering constructions.

**Status:** EXCELLENT

**Evidence:**
