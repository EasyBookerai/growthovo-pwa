### Enhancement Pattern Established
Each enhanced lesson includes:
- **Concept Card (75-100 words):** Surprising hook, specific mechanisms, memorable metaphors
- **Example Card (75-100 words):** Real character, clear transformation, emotional payoff
- **Mistake Card (75-100 words):** WHY it fails, compassionate tone, alternative approach
- **Science Card (75-100 words):** Institution cited, numbers, mechanism explained
- **Challenge Card (50-75 words):** Immediate action, specific duration, reflection prompt
