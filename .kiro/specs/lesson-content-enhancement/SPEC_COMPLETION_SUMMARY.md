## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well
1. **Sample-first approach**: Creating 5 samples for approval saved rework
2. **Quality checklists**: Systematic review prevented issues
3. **Pillar-by-pillar enhancement**: Manageable chunks maintained quality
4. **Documentation-as-we-go**: Made handoff seamless

### Process Improvements for Next Time
1. **SQL automation**: Invest in robust markdown-to-SQL converter upfront
2. **Earlier inclusivity review**: Integrate into initial enhancement phase
3. **Iterative user testing**: Deploy subsets to test groups before full rollout
